You have two ropes of equal length and a lighter. If you light a rope from one of the ends, it will take exactly one hour to fully burn.
However, the ropes burn unevenly. If half of a rope has burned, you don't know that half the time has passed (it might take, for example, 1 minute to burn 90% of the rope, and 59 minutes to burn the last 10%).

How can you precisely measure out 45 mintues?

<Hint>
Can you figure out a way to measure 30 minutes?
</Hint>

<Solution>
Note that if you start a rope burning at both ends, it will take exactly 30 minutes to burn through.

Start the first rope burning at both ends, and the second rope burning at one end.
After the first rope burns through, 30 minutes have passed, and there are 30 minutes remaining on the second rope.
Light the second rope on the non-burning end. Now it will take only 15 minutes to finish burning.

When the second rope finishes burning, 45 minutes have passed.
</Solution>
